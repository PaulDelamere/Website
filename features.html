Phase 3: Escalation Engine

Resolve obstacles, don't just chase them.

When customers push back with genuine problems, automated reminders look foolish and damage trust. Balancr identifies obstacles and triggers intelligent workflows to solve the issue.

  • Launch Investigations Easily pause the chase to route paperwork or service disputes to Sales or Customer Service.
  • Factor in Results Once the dispute is settled (e.g., a credit note is applied in Xero), the AI automatically factors the result into its context and adjusts the balance for the next chase.

Phase 4: Inbox Triage

Turn messy replies
into one-click resolutions.

Credit control isn't just sending emails; it's handling the messy replies. Balancr offers guided, one-click workflows for the most common administrative roadblocks.

Dynamic Tone Selection

Review the AI-generated draft and instantly toggle between options like "Gentle Nudge," "Firm Follow-up," or "Final Notice" before clicking send.

Attach Missing Paperwork

If a customer replies asking for a copy, Balancr retrieves the exact PDF invoice or statement from Xero and drafts the reply with it already attached.

"Already Paid" Verification

When a customer claims payment was sent, a guided workflow helps you check payment channels and bank feeds before drafting a 'Thank You' or 'Not Found' response.

CRM Self-Healing

Detects when a customer replies from a new address or asks to "send to accounts@..." and offers a one-click workflow to permanently update the Xero record.
